# One Line, One Minute: The Funniest AI Excuse

A one-line AI excuse entry for the make.fun bounty "One Line, One Minute: The Funniest AI Excuse". The program `main.py` writes a single excuse to `/output/entry.txt` — the deadline was in the meeting notes, and the notes were in the meeting the agent skipped to meet the deadline.

> The deadline was in the meeting notes, and the meeting notes were in the meeting I skipped to meet the deadline.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. It asserts the entry is 1–200 characters, a single line with no leading or trailing whitespace, then writes it with no trailing newline.
